Privacy Policy

Effective: November 16, 2026 / Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains how kuwabaky ("we") handles information in the iPhone app "ひとじ" (Hitoji, "the app"). The Japanese version of this policy is the authoritative one.

1. Information the app handles

Your answers and result are not sent to us or to anyone else. The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, date of birth, precise location or contacts. There are no accounts.

  • Saved on your iPhone: your answers to the 12 questions (the number of the answer you chose for each question), the kanji you chose on the result screen, and the date and time you finished answering.
  • Collected by Google when ads are shown: device identifiers (the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and an identifier used only within our apps), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and OS information, records of ads you saw or tapped, records of app launches and interactions, and crash and performance data.

If you use iPhone backups (iCloud Backup, or backups to a Mac or PC), the information saved on your iPhone is included in those backups. Backups are between you and Apple; we cannot see them.

2. Purpose

  • To show your result (the type of your year and the kanji)
  • To let you continue, or see your earlier result, after closing the app
  • To show ads and choose ads that may suit you (if you allow tracking, your activity in other companies' apps and websites is also used) and to measure them (done by Google)

3. Ads

The app shows ads from Google LLC (Google AdMob): a banner at the top and at the bottom of the question and result screens, and a full-screen ad. The full-screen ad is shown after you finish the 12 questions and see the result, when you leave the result screen with "16の型を見る" (See the 16 types) or "もう一度答える" (Answer again), at most once per completed set. To show them, Google's software in the app (Google Mobile Ads SDK) sends the information listed above to Google. Your answers, result and chosen kanji are not sent. See Google's Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Advertising.

Before the first ad is loaded (when you first tap "はじめる" (Start) and the first question appears), iOS asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites (App Tracking Transparency). Every feature of the app works either way. If you do not allow it, ads are still shown; the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used and you are not tracked across other companies' apps and websites. Google may still choose ads using an identifier that works only within our apps (Google's publisher first-party ID) and records of ads and interactions in this app; Google states that this identifier cannot link your activity to other companies' apps (First-party identifiers and data). You can change tracking at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, turn off ad selection based on that identifier through the mark in the corner of the ad, and manage ad settings for your Google Account in My Ad Center. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages (Google's "G" rating). To report an inappropriate ad, see the support page.

4. Sending information outside the app

Apart from the ads described above, the app does not send your information. Other than the ads software, it uses no analytics or crash-reporting services.

When you use "共有する" (Share) or "画像を保存" (Save image), the card image goes to the destination you choose (Messages, a social app, Photos). The image contains the year, the kanji, the type name, the four words of the type, the season group, three lines (two lines decided by your answers and one wish for next year), and the app name and fixed headings. It does not contain your name or the full list of your answers.

For sharing, the app creates the card image in a temporary location on your iPhone. It is deleted when you close the card screen or when you use "答えを消す" (Delete answers).

When you open this page or the support page from the app, your browser connects to this site (GitHub Pages). Your answers and result are not sent.

5. Transfers outside Japan

Google LLC is a company in the United States, and the information collected for ads may be stored and processed on servers in the United States and elsewhere. For the data protection system of the United States, see the report published by Japan's Personal Information Protection Commission (in Japanese). Google protects the information under Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Photos

When you tap "画像を保存" (Save image), the app asks for permission to add to your Photos. The app only adds the image; it does not read your photos. You can change the permission at any time in Settings.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Your answers and chosen kanji stay on your iPhone until you delete them.
  • In the app, tap the (i) button at the top right > "答えを消す" (Delete answers) to delete that year's answers and result. This cannot be undone.
  • Deleting the app from your iPhone deletes the information saved in it.
  • Copies already included in a backup can remain there even after you delete your answers in the app or delete the app. To remove them, you need to delete that backup where it is stored: iCloud backups from Settings on your iPhone (iCloud storage management), and backups on a Mac or PC from Manage Backups in the Finder or the Apple Devices app on that computer (see Apple Support for the steps).
  • We do not hold your answers or result, so there is nothing for us to delete. Information collected by Google for ads is kept according to Google's policies (we only receive totals such as the number of ads shown).

8. Children

Anyone can use the app, but it is not directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should use it with a parent's consent. The app does not send anyone's answers or result, including children's, to us.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. If the information the app handles changes (for example, if a new feature sends information outside the app), we will announce it on this page beforehand. Changes take effect when posted here.
