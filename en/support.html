Support

Support for the iPhone app "ひとじ" (Hitoji). The app is in Japanese.

How is the result decided?

Only by your answers to the 12 questions. The app counts your answers on four axes and the combination gives one of 16 types. Nothing is random: the same answers always give the same result. The result screen shows the tally of your answers.

How do I delete my answers?

Tap the (i) button at the top right of the start or result screen, then "答えを消す" (Delete answers). This cannot be undone.

I cannot save the image

The first time you tap "画像を保存" (Save image), the app asks for permission to add to Photos. If you declined, allow it in Settings > Apps > ひとじ > Photos. "共有する" (Share) works without that permission.

About ads

A banner is shown at the top and at the bottom of the question and result screens. After you finish the 12 questions and see the result, one full-screen ad is shown when you tap "16の型を見る" (See the 16 types) or "もう一度答える" (Answer again) on the result screen (at most once per completed set; closing it takes you on). It is never shown before the result or while viewing, saving or sending the card. The card image never contains ads.

To report an inappropriate ad: tap the small mark in the corner of the ad (an "i" or a triangle) to report it to Google or stop seeing it. If that does not work, email us below with the date and time and what the ad showed (a screenshot helps). We will block that ad.

To change how ads are chosen: turning off tracking for this app in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking stops tracking across other companies' apps and websites. Ad selection based on records within our apps can be turned off through the mark in the corner of the ad. Ad settings for your Google Account are in Google's My Ad Center.
