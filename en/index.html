一年の一字

Ichinen no Ichiji — one kanji for your year, from 12 questions.

Answer 12 questions about what you actually did this year. The app shows one of 16 types of year and one kanji written with a brush, and makes a card image you can share. The app is in Japanese.

An iPhone app. We are preparing its release on the App Store.

Your data

Your answers and result are saved on your iPhone. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. If you use iPhone backups (such as iCloud), they are included in those backups. The app has no accounts, no ads and no purchases.
